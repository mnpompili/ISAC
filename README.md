ISAC: Identification of Synchronously Active Cells
A tool to detect Cell Assemblies in high density extra-cellular electrophysiology recordings

ISAC is a method specifically designed to detect groups of neurons that fire together above chance and performs a rigorous iterative search across all recorded units, leveraging the full dimensionality of the dataset to maximize statistical power.

Rather than use pairwise correlations as a proxy measure of cellular co-activations, ISAC directly searches for and detects groups of cells firing together above random chance levels. The resulting patterns are composed of members that are recurrently active together, ensuring that individual groups are not erroneous amalgams
51 of distinct assemblies. Furthermore, ISAC conducts an iterative search for all possible combinations of neurons within a recording, ensuring the detection of virtually all significantly active groups of synchronously active cells in a recording.
